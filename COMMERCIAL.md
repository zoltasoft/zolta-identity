# Community core and commercial extensions

Zolta Identity's community core is the complete code in this repository and is licensed under Apache-2.0. It can be self-hosted and used commercially under that license.

Commercial or Enterprise extensions, if offered, are separate packages with their own source repositories, release channels, and commercial terms. They are not part of this repository, are not required to run the community core, and are not granted by the Apache-2.0 license.

The community core will continue to receive security fixes. Enterprise offerings may provide additional governance, scale, compliance, support, or operational capabilities; they do not change the license for this repository.
